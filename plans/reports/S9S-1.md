# S9S-1: Surface step-over on curves; repair patch reach

**Status:** done (both tasks). Branch `wp/S9S`.

## Done
1. **Step-over along the surface** (`planner/surface.py`, to-do 5). New `arc_map(mesh, across, along)`:
   cuts the region with 7 planes normal to `along`, measures cut length per unit of flat
   distance along `across`, averages, and integrates to a flat → surface-distance map. Returns
   None when the region is flat (stretch within 1e-6 of 1), so flat picks keep the old offsets
   bit-for-bit.
   - Zigzag (`surface_strokes`, strong mean normal only): cut offsets are placed every
     `step_over` of surface distance, mapped back to flat offsets.
   - Pattern fill (`pattern_strokes`): the flattened region is segmentized and stretched on
     both flat axes before filling; each stroke is resampled in the stretched frame and mapped
     back before the rays drop it onto the faces.
   - Test `test_surface.py::test_step_over_measured_along_the_surface_over_a_fillet[zigzag|oneway]`:
     a 40 mm flat + R20 fillet (60 deg) strip; gaps between passes measured by arc length are
     within 5 % of step_over. Both fail on main.
2. **Repair patch top + 4 sides** (to-do 2): the cause was not the lift height. Lowering
   `jump_height_mm` to 10 and `approach_height_mm` to 20 left the same 10 moves out of reach.
   Those 10 moves (turns and the face-to-face jump on the side faces) had the gun rolled 90 deg about
   its spray axis, because the pose's travel direction was the step across (vertical on a side
   face). Turns and face-to-face jumps now use the next stroke's tangent as the travel, so the
   gun keeps the next stroke's roll. Auto-position now finds reach 1.0 (tilt 0, roll -180,
   E1 2500) with 0 unreachable moves at the default lifts. xfail removed from
   `test_surface_e2e::test_repair_patch_top_and_sides`.
   - Side effect: on every Surface path, turn moves now hold the next stroke's roll, not a roll 90 deg off it
     (this was true on flat faces too). Region-to-region jumps are unchanged.
