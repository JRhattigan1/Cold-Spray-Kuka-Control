## Done
- Labels: each plane has a flat `pv.Text3D` label ("XY", "XZ", "YZ") in its colour. The labels are in `Datums.labels[k]`. Each label lies in its plane, in the (+u, +v) corner, and its height is 6% of the plane size. Its `user_matrix` is the plane's matrix @ `label_matrix(name, size)`, so it follows the frame, the offset and the size. `set_visible` shows and hides the label with its plane. Labels are never pickable, so `name_of` and picking are unchanged.
- `Datums.set_size(mm)` changes the edge length of all three squares. It rebuilds the plane mesh in place (`mapper.dataset.copy_from`) and re-places the labels. The default is still 600 mm (`Datums.size`).
- Test: `tests/test_datums.py::test_datum_labels_and_size` checks that labels toggle with their plane, are not pickable, follow the offset, sit inside the square, scale with size, and that `set_size` resizes the planes.

- The label scale is set in the matrix rather than rebuilding the text, so `set_size` costs almost nothing for the labels.
