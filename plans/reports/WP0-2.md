## Decisions & deviations from the plan
- Str fields such as `turn_plane` are hidden QLineEdits rather than left out of `self.fields`, so `read_process()` stays generic and keeps their value. A later WP can give them a proper picker (for example, a datum plane combo).
- CylFace.axis is the OCC axis direction and is not flipped for face orientation. Inside and outside (bore or outer surface) is not recorded. Tell them apart by the triangle normals, or by radius.
- `CylFace.point` is the OCC axis location (for test_tube it is on z=0; for test_block's hole, z=-1). It is not necessarily the face's centre.
