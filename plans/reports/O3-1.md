# O3-1: vectorised IK + base frame cache

**Status:** done.

**Done:**
- `Robot._candidates`: the OPW closed form runs on all poses at once and returns (n, 24, 6): 8 branches × A6 −360/0/+360, with limits applied (NaN where a branch is unreachable or out of limits).
- `ik_path` chooses the nearest branch to the previous joints in a cheap loop. Hint/seed semantics are unchanged.
- `ik` and `ik_all` share the same code.
- `sim/viewer.py set_path` computes `base_world` once per distinct axes tuple, and `robroot` and its inverse once per E1 value.

**Benchmark** (WearPlate2 Surface, 43,267 samples, targets + ik_path):
- 23.95 s before (11.28 s targets + 12.67 s IK);
- 3.32 s after (0.99 s + 2.33 s);
- the joints are identical: same NaN pattern, allclose 1e-6.

**Not done:** the collision `_place` cache, which isn't owned here.
