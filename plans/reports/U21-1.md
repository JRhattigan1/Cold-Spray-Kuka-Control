# U21 window scaling and positions (sprint 1)

Status: in progress, sprint 1 committed (c0647a3).

Done
- Sidebar and 3D view now share the body through a QSplitter (app.py): transparent 14 px handle in the gap, the 3D view takes window resizes, the sidebar keeps its width, and the user can drag it wider. Neither side collapses.
- The panels no longer have a fixed 380 px width. The sidebar starts at PANEL_WIDTH 460 and can't go below max(PANEL_MIN 400, the widest panel's minimumSizeHint + scroll bar + margins). Horizontal scroll is off, so forms reflow to the width.
- Start size: fit_size() caps 1600x950 at 92 % of the primary screen's available area. Qt 6 already scales logical px per monitor DPI, so no DPI flags are needed.
- Strategy mode capsule shows "Layers / Revolve / Surface". The full MODES text is the button tooltip (toolpath.py short_label).
- tests/test_gui_layout.py: 5 tests (short labels, fit_size, capsule labels, splitter resize/widen, panels fit the sidebar).

Not done
- Splitter width is not persisted in QSettings.
- Step bar: the done-step labels ("1 Part", "2 Position") look faint or blurred in the grab() screenshots. This may come from nested QGraphicsEffects (capsule shadow + pill glow). Needs checking on a real display.
