# S9J-1: job save / open

**Status:** done.

## Done
- `src/coldspray/job_io.py`: versioned job dict (`version: 1`), YAML `*.csjob`. `save_job` / `load_job` / `normalize`. Missing keys take their defaults and unknown keys are ignored. A STEP source is also stored relative to the job file and used when the absolute path no longer exists.
- Contents: the part source (STEP path or built-in name), body roles, build body, start and base faces, coat faces (Surface), KRL base and its face; mount (fixed in cell), exact placement 4x4, keep on plate, substrate, nozzle, tilt, rotation, gantry E1, gun angle (roll), constraint rows (kind, faces, ref, value, suppressed, fix pose); mode, coats and every ProcessConfig field; purge config; export pre-flight.
- The solver-applied set-up is covered: Apply suggested writes the E1 and purge widgets, and the job saves those.
- `src/coldspray/gui/job_menu.py`: `collect(win)`, `apply(win, job)`, save/open dialogs, and a "Job" button in the step bar (Save job..., Open job..., up to 6 recent jobs in QSettings `jobs/recent`), with Ctrl+S / Ctrl+O. Opening restores every step and ends on Toolpath; the user presses Generate.
- app.py: 2 lines (import + `job_menu.install(self)`). part.py: 1 line (`self.part_source`).
- KRL output is unchanged.

## Not done
- The cell's gantry E1 when the cell has no gantry, and the Show-group checkboxes (view only), are not saved.
- Nothing is regenerated automatically on open, by design.
