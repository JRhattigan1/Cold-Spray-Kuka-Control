# S9T-1: predicted coating thickness, cycle time and powder estimates

**Status:** done (all 3 tasks). Branch wp/S9T, not pushed.

## Done
- `src/coldspray/sim/deposit.py` (new):
  - Gaussian track footprint about the spray axis (tool X). **track_width_mm = FWHM**: sigma = w / 2.355.
  - Calibrated so one layer of passes at `step_over_mm` and `traverse_speed_mm_s` gives `layer_thickness_mm`: peak rate A = L·V·s / (2π σ²).
  - Each sample's deposit is rate × dt. dt is the distance to the sample over the move speed; dwells of spray-on moves and `spray_settle_s` are added where they happen.
  - Cosine factor on tilted faces. A steady layer keeps its thickness (mass is conserved) and a single track gets wider and lower. Back faces get nothing.
  - Not modelled: shadowing, standoff falloff, deposition efficiency vs angle.
  - The mesh is refined (split along the longest edge) to edges ≤ 1.5σ. Thickness is per triangle.
  - `Deposit.stats()` gives area-weighted min, mean and max over the sprayed area (> 10% of max), plus the % of that area below 80% of the mean.
  - Fully vectorised: the neighbour search uses cKDTree `sparse_distance_matrix`, in chunks. test_tube revolve takes about 4 s (137k triangles, 27k samples). The test plate takes about 1 s.
  - `estimate(path, proc)`: cycle time uses the same model as `estimate_time_s`, and the test asserts they are equal. Spray-on time includes travel, settle and dwells. Powder is spray-on time × `powder_feed_g_min`, with no deposition efficiency applied.
- `ProcessConfig.powder_feed_g_min` = 30.0 g/min, advanced, with a comment giving the typical range of 10-60 g/min. It was also added to config/process.yaml.
- `sim/viewer.py`: new `Scene.set_thickness(deposit|None)` only. It adds a separate viridis actor with a "Thickness (mm)" scalar bar, offset 0.2 mm off the translucent part, and it rides with the faceplate. Path drawing is untouched.
- Simulate step:
  - New "Thickness" check box under the summary. It computes the thickness on demand (wait cursor), caches it per toolpath and shows the min/mean/max line and the below-80% line.
  - It is disabled in Layers mode. A new toolpath unticks it.
  - The summary gains "Cycle ≈ … · spray on … · powder ≈ … g".
- Export step: the same estimate line shows above "Save KRL program...", set in `_show_program`.
- gui/app.py was not touched.
