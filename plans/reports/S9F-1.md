# S9F-1: collision e2e "A2/A3 hit Filtration unit on every point"

**Status:** done. The cause is the IK, not the model. There is one real bug fix (E1 round-off crash in the checker) and one new passing test.

## Root cause (evidence)
- **The model is consistent.** All 7 robot CAD instances have the matrix `robroot` gives at E1=0, to 0.01 mm. The robot meshes are in the root frame. At home, nothing hits at E1 = 0 / 1000 / 4000, and the CAD-pose learned contacts are "none".
- **The Filtration unit is where the CAD puts it**: bounding box X -1308..1302, Y 437..1540, Z 0..2830. It is one COMPOUND plus a panel at Y 720. It sits under the gantry, behind the robot (robot root Y 1069, Z 3196; the A2 joint is at Y 494, Z 3022). `purge.suggest_purge` deliberately sprays into it, so it is part of the design. The minimum distance from the arm at home is 128 mm (A1).
- **Sample 2991 of test_block** (E1 2000, E2 90) has the greedy joints [-4.8, 11.7, -74.2, -168.8, -17.8, -7.3]. This is elbow-down: the elbow is at world Y 755, Z 1763, deep inside the unit's footprint. 7389 contact points between A2/A3 and the Filtration unit fall within X -598..507, Y 634..801, Z 1618..2758.
- **There are collision-free solutions for the same TCP**: [-4.8, -55.4, 79.8, 176.4, 69.5, 4.7] (elbow-up) and [175.2, -142, -41.6, ...].
- **Why greedy picks elbow-down:** `Robot.ik_path` / `ik` rank solutions by max-abs joint distance from the seed (home). Elbow-up needs a wrist flip of about 175 deg in A4 or A6, while elbow-down needs A4 -169. So elbow-down "wins" and the whole path stays there (A2 is +10..12 over all samples).
- **The arm lever fixes it.** `solver.solve` with the real checker reports "arm: 2839 of 2841 problem points fixed". Re-checked with `hint=path.hint_for(...)`, as Simulate does, the result is 0 collisions out of 5982 samples.

## Real hits that remain and the levers that would fix them
- **block zigzag, greedy:** arm lever (done by Solve). A cheaper change would be to weight the `ik_path` distance (for example A1-A3 over the wrist, or sum instead of max), so that the greedy choice and the KRL post seed elbow-up. It was not changed because it affects the solver golden and the KRL regression tests.
- **block + purge:** the purge point (-884.56, 400, 2000) is 37 mm in front of the Filtration unit face (Y 437). With the default spray direction, the gun and nozzle enter the unit. Fixes: the purge lever, or `suggest_purge` (standoff 150 mm). The approach moves at E1 2500 hit the faceplate with A3: arm lever / reroute.
- **block + purge, after solving** (E1 2500): "arm: 2907 of 3551 fixed". The 2586 greedy hit points drop to 535, and all of them are on `purge` moves: gun, nozzle and A4-A6 vs the Filtration unit, plus nozzle and A4 vs the faceplate. The purge lever made no change, so the lever needed is to move the purge point (suggest_purge, standoff 150) or to fix the purge example in the test / cell.yaml.
- **repair surface** (E1 2500): the greedy run has 657 of 2681 points hit, mostly the wrist, gun and nozzle vs the faceplate, plus nozzle vs fixture and A2/A3 vs the Filtration unit. The solver applied only "gantry: 17 of 981 fixed", suggesting E1 3250, and the 2 mm re-check is worse (883 points). The arm lever found nothing, so these are real reach-around hits over a tilted faceplate. They need angle, lean or reroute (none of which helped here) or a different tilt / roll at the auto_position stage, for example making auto_position collision-aware.
  - Possible solver issue: the gantry lever's 5 mm find_issues scoring disagrees with the 2 mm check.
