- `distance` uses |offset| along b's normal (either side) and accepts parallel or anti-parallel normals.
- Rotation is weighted 100 mm/rad against translation in the parameters and in the `fix` residual.
- Default pivot is the part-CAD origin so the result matches `constrain()`; A3 may pass the part centroid as `pivot` for a more natural "smallest move".
- Conflict reporting: per-constraint residuals > tol show which rows to mark red; with a real conflict LM splits the error between the conflicting rows.
